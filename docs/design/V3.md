# V3: a structured course, not a dashboard

Owner feedback (2026-11-10): lessons feel random and assume knowledge ("90% of it I don't know"), and the site went
from calm to chaotic. V3 replaces `REDESIGN.md` where they conflict.

## What went wrong

1. **No ladder.** The 20 lessons are ~300-word interview cheat-sheets (e.g. Hashing opens with load factor and the
   Equals/GetHashCode contract). Nothing teaches what an array, a table or a server is first. Prerequisites exist in
   `content/workspaces/*.json` but nothing below the 20 topics exists to point to.
2. **No order you can see.** Learning Path lists 20 topics by domain; the order is a number hidden in JSON.
3. **Reading is a wall.** A lesson is one long page plus four tabs (Read, Practice, Exercises, Check). Nothing asks
   you anything while you read, so it's passive and boring.
4. **Too many surfaces.** Seven nav items. Home is either the mentor page or a 12-section dashboard (skill path, plan,
   focus, chart, revision, proficient, recent activity, radar...). Dotted hero, skill trail, chamfered buttons and five
   domain colours all compete.

## Principles (borrowed from tutoring sites that work)

- **Brilliant:** one idea per screen, and you *do* something every screen or two (a quick check), so reading is active.
- **Duolingo:** a visible path of small lessons, a clear "Continue" button, a streak, and a small celebration on finish.
- **Khan Academy:** start from zero, prerequisites before depth, mastery is earned by checks, not by reading.
- **Calm UI:** one reading column, one accent colour, lots of space, at most three things on the home screen.

## Curriculum

`content/curriculum.json` is the single source of order: track (one per domain) → three stages (Foundations, Core,
Interview depth) → topics. Every lesson appears exactly once. The 20 existing topics keep their keys (progress, SRS and
the mentor depend on them); 23 new beginner topics fill the ladder below them. Order within a stage is the teaching
order. Nothing is locked: the path *suggests*, it never blocks.

## Lesson format

A lesson is markdown with `title` and `summary` frontmatter. Each `## ` heading starts a new **step** (one screen).
New lessons follow this shape (existing lessons keep their content but gain the first step):

1. `## The idea` — plain words and an everyday analogy. No jargon that hasn't been explained. ~120 words.
2. `## See it work` — a small worked example traced step by step (C# for code, ≤ 15 lines; ASCII diagrams welcome).
3. `## How it works` — the mechanics, introducing the real terms.
4. `## Where you'll meet it` — real systems/products/interview problems where this shows up.
5. `## Common mistakes` — 2–4 traps.
6. `## Recap` — 3–5 bullets.

Steps 2–5 may be split or renamed if the topic needs it; keep 5–8 steps, 600–1000 words total.

**Quick checks** sit inside a step as a fenced block, between 2 and 4 per lesson:

````
```check
{"prompt": "…", "options": ["…", "…", "…"], "answer": 0, "explanation": "…"}
```
````

They are for engagement only (not stored, not scored). The real **checkpoint** at the end of a lesson uses the topic's
questions in `content/questions/<domain>.json` (stored, scored, scheduled for review as today).

## Lesson player (`/learn/<domain>/<topic>/`)

- Steps shown one at a time as a card, with a thin progress bar and "Continue". A quick check must be answered before
  Continue; it then shows right/wrong and the explanation (no penalty).
- After the last step: **Checkpoint** (the topic's questions, practice mode, easiest first), then a **Lesson complete**
  screen: a small celebration (respect `prefers-reduced-motion`), what you learned (the recap), and "Next lesson →".
- Below the player, secondary and collapsed: Exercises, Practical work, Sources. `?tab=` links from today's plan keep
  working (map them to the nearest view).
- Reading time still counts via `LessonReadTracker`.

## Learn (`/learn/` and `/learn/<domain>/`)

- `/learn/`: five track cards (title, tagline, "n of m lessons done", a thin bar) and one "Continue" call to action.
- `/learn/<domain>/`: a vertical path of nodes grouped by stage. Node states: **done** (proficiency ≥ practising —
  lesson read and checkpoint taken), **current** (first not-done topic), **upcoming**. Each node: title, summary,
  ~minutes. The current node is visually prominent with a "Start"/"Continue" button.

## Today (`/`)

Three things only:
1. **Continue** card: the next lesson (the current topic of the track touched most recently; a new learner starts at
   DSA → Big-O basics), with track progress.
2. **Review** card: questions due (link to `/practice/`), hidden when zero.
3. **Streak** line: days in a row with any lesson step finished or question answered, and today's goal (1 lesson or
   10 questions).

A single quiet line links to the mentor's note when one exists. Everything else on the old dashboard is removed from
home (radar, charts, focus lists). Mentor, Coding and Radar keep their own pages.

## Navigation

Top bar / phone tab bar: **Today · Learn · Practice · More**. "More" holds Coding, Mentor, Radar, Fun corner, Settings.

## Visual system

- One accent (blue). Domain colours only as a small dot next to a domain name. No dotted hero, no skill trail, no
  chamfered buttons.
- Reading column `max-w-2xl` for lessons, `max-w-3xl` for pages. Base text 16–17px, line-height 1.65.
- Cards: `surface` background, 1px `border`, radius 12px, no heavy shadow. Spacing scale 4/8/12/16/24/32/48.
- Light and dark themes stay WCAG AA (`tests/design.test.ts`).
- Motion: short (150–250ms) ease-out for step changes and the completion pop; none under reduced motion.
