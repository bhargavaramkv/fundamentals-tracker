## What happened

On **21 Oct 2026** the PHP project shipped emergency releases for two critical
flaws in the **core interpreter**, CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619
(CVSS v4 9.2), nicknamed **PHP2Shell**. Both allow **unauthenticated remote
code execution** on a server that serves PHP. CISA added both to its Known
Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on **26 Oct**, after exploitation was seen in
the wild. Unit 42 reported over 22 million internet-exposed instances that could
be affected.

- **Affected:** every PHP setup that serves web requests: Apache `mod_php`
  (rated highest risk), PHP-FPM, php-cgi, FrankenPHP, LiteSpeed.
- **Fixed in:** **8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36, 8.2.35**.
- **Follow-up:** a separate, lower-risk flaw (CVE-2026-115560) affects unusual
  session setups. PHP advises disabling `session.upload_progress` until a
  routine fix ships.

## Why it matters to you

You don't write PHP, but PHP is probably *running* somewhere near you: a
WordPress marketing site, an internal admin tool, a vendor appliance (Cisco
published its own advisory), or a `php:*` base image in a Kubernetes namespace
nobody owns. An RCE in one forgotten pod becomes a foothold in the cluster.

## The fundamentals behind it

| Fundamental | How it applies here |
|---|---|
| **Patch triage** | Severity (CVSS) says how bad it *could* be; KEV says it's being exploited *now*. KEV plus internet-facing jumps the queue. |
| **Inventory / SBOM** | You can't patch what you can't find. Image scanning (Trivy, Defender for Containers) answers "where is PHP running?" in minutes. |
| **Defense in depth** | Non-root containers, read-only root filesystems, NetworkPolicies and no cluster-admin service accounts turn an RCE into a contained incident. |
| **Unauthenticated attack surface** | Auth middleware can't protect you from bugs that trigger *before* your code runs, in the runtime itself. |

## What to do

1. Find PHP: scan registries and running images, and check `Server`/`X-Powered-By` headers on internal hosts.
2. Upgrade to a fixed version, and rebuild images (don't just restart pods).
3. Check for compromise: unexpected processes, outbound connections, new files in web roots.
